# TWYN — Brand Direction A

**Chosen 2026-08-15.** Source of truth for every TWYN surface (landing, app, video, social).

## Logo system
- **Hero lockup:** particle twin-mark (two overlapping dot-globes, glowing intersection) above wide-tracked TWYN wordmark
- **Secondary:** wordmark alone, letter-spacing ~0.45em
- **App icons:** particle venn · particle ring · TWYN caps · twyn lowercase (rounded-square tiles on black)

## Copy lockup
```
TWYN
NOT AN ASSISTANT. A TWIN.
Your memory. Your voice. Your judgment.
```
- Headline style: "Perceptive by design. Built for you."
- Body voice: "TWYN is not here to answer for you. It's here to remember with you, think with you, and stand with you."

## Typography
- **Primary:** Suisse Int'l / Neue Haas Grotesk (licensed; free fallback: Inter/system-ui). Clean. Modern. Human.
- **Secondary (data/system):** IBM Plex Mono / SF Mono. Data. System. Precision.
- Sublines run letterspaced caps; system/meta lines run mono.

## Tone of voice
Calm · Precise · Human · Observant · Reflective (alt set: Intimate · Perceptive · Steady · Thoughtful · Lucid)

## Visual language
Particle everything on pure black (#000): waves, eclipse ring, voice waveform, nebulae. White particles, accent #8B7CFF reserved for interactive elements. No emoji-as-icons, no gradients-on-cards, no light mode.

## UI application patterns
- Voice: waveform line through a particle ring + "Listening…"
- Chat: TWYN avatar (mark), typing dots, question-forward copy ("How can I help you understand yourself better?")
- Statement cards: particle nebula left, short reflective copy right
